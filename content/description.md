Notes on LiDAR: how the sensor turns the flight time of laser light into a cloud of 3D points, and the algorithms that turn those points into maps, poses, objects and tracks. The [crash course](./sequences/lidar-crash-course/) is a short textbook meant to be read in order, from the physics of a single laser pulse to registration, SLAM and learned 3D detectors. Alongside it, the site collects writeups of recent research papers, each linked back to the part of the course it builds on.
