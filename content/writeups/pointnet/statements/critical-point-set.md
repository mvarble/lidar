---
type: statement
kind: theorem
title: Critical point sets
slug: critical-point-set
---

Let $u : \calX \to \bbR^K$ be $u(S) = \operatorname{MAX}_{\bfx_i \in S}\{h(\bfx_i)\}$ and let $f = \gamma \circ u$. Then

1. for every $S$ there are sets $C_S \subseteq S \subseteq N_S$ such that $f(T) = f(S)$ for every $T$ with $C_S \subseteq T \subseteq N_S$, and
2. $|C_S| \le K$.
